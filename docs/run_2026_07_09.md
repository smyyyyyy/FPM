## Notes

- Structured one-shot was not effective on this first-50 pilot: it produced many `UNKNOWN` labels and did not reduce false positives.
- The iterative smoke test successfully executed CodeQL evidence templates after adding a temporary `qlpack.yml` and local `additional-packs` resolution.
- For the first iterative sample, the final label changed from one-shot `UNKNOWN` to iterative `TP`, showing the evidence loop is operational.
- Template queries are still preliminary and should be refined before a full paper-grade run.
